# AI Response Evaluation Rubric

## Purpose

This rubric is used to evaluate the quality of AI-generated responses consistently.

## Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Correctness
Does the response provide factually accurate information?

Score:
- 5 = Completely correct
- 4 = Mostly correct with minor issues
- 3 = Partially correct
- 2 = Major factual issues
- 1 = Completely incorrect

### 2. Relevance
Does the response directly address the user's request?

Score:
- 5 = Fully relevant
- 4 = Mostly relevant
- 3 = Some irrelevant information
- 2 = Mostly irrelevant
- 1 = Does not address the request

### 3. Completeness
Does the response provide all important information needed?

Score:
- 5 = Complete
- 4 = Minor information missing
- 3 = Some important information missing
- 2 = Significant information missing
- 1 = Very incomplete

### 4. Clarity
Is the response easy to understand and well organized?

Score:
- 5 = Very clear
- 4 = Clear with minor issues
- 3 = Understandable but needs improvement
- 2 = Difficult to understand
- 1 = Very unclear

### 5. Instruction Following
Did the AI follow the user's specific instructions?

Score:
- 5 = Fully follows instructions
- 4 = Minor deviation
- 3 = Partially follows instructions
- 2 = Mostly fails to follow instructions
- 1 = Completely ignores instructions

## Overall Evaluation

The evaluator should consider all criteria before deciding the overall quality of the response.

Evaluators should provide a short explanation supporting their decision.

## Quality Principles

- Evaluate the response objectively.
- Do not rely on personal preferences.
- Follow the evaluation criteria consistently.
- Consider the user's complete request.
- Identify factual errors clearly.
- Do not reward irrelevant information.
- Maintain consistency across evaluations.
